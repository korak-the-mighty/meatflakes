# EU regulatory workstream

**Source review: 5 October 2026.** This is an issue map, not legal advice or confirmation that any SKU may be sold. Applicable obligations depend on composition, process, food category, facility, country, and consumer instructions. Recheck the current consolidated text and consult the competent authority or qualified adviser before launch.

## Required classification decisions

Identify whether each SKU is ready to eat as sold, whether hot-food use is just serving or a necessary safety step, how the meat product is classified, whether curing or additives change applicable rules, and what name accurately describes guanciale-derived pieces. Confirm national implementation and authority expectations for the first launch country. A recipe instruction to sprinkle onto hot pasta cannot substitute for a validated safety process.

## Label and claims review

Check species naming, ingredient order, allergens from seasonings, nutrition declaration, net quantity, date marking, storage before and after opening, responsible business details, origin rules, and any voluntary claims. “High protein,” “no preservatives,” “natural,” “shelf stable,” and similar messages need SKU-specific support and applicable EU claim review. No draft label is approved here.

## Evidence to assemble

Facility status; supplier specifications; HACCP documentation; process validation; microbial and shelf-life data; pack specification; traceability and recall procedure; final artwork; and review of each intended market. See [shelf-life plan](SHELF_LIFE.md) for the evidence plan.
