# Shelf-life and safety evidence plan

**No Meatflakes shelf life has been established.** Unopened ambient storage for **12–24+ months is an ambition to test, not a claim or provisional date mark**. A shorter life may be the only feasible outcome. Do not sell, ship, or leave test meat at room temperature based on visual dryness or a single water-activity reading.

## Relevant established guidance

- The [FDA's water-activity guide](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/inspection-technical-guides/water-activity-aw-foods) explains how available water affects microbial growth. Its regulatory examples concern US law and specific food classes; they are not a universal approval threshold.
- The [USDA FSIS jerky guidance](https://fsistraining.fsis.usda.gov/mod/resource/view.php?id=1852) distinguishes pathogen reduction from shelf stability and discusses packaging and water activity for jerky. It is a useful technical comparator, not a process validation for flakes or EU compliance guidance.
- [EU Regulation 2073/2005](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02005R2073-20260701) sets microbiological criteria for relevant food categories and defines ready-to-eat food. A product intended to be sprinkled directly onto meals needs category-specific review.
- [Germany's BfR describes fat oxidation and rancid flavor as a possible meat shelf-life limiter](https://www.bfr.bund.de/en/press-release/no-threat-to-consumers-from-cops-from-packaged-meat/). This supports testing quality deterioration as well as microbes; it does not predict Meatflakes' shelf life.

## Hazards and quality failures to assess

| Area | Why it matters | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pathogens | Drying can leave surviving organisms; later handling can recontaminate food | Validated kill step, hygienic post-process controls, appropriate testing |
| Moisture heterogeneity | Thick or fatty pieces may dry unevenly; package leaks may cause moisture uptake | Sampling plan across pieces, lots, and packages; water-activity testing over time |
| Oxidation | Meat fat, especially in guanciale, can develop rancid flavors | Sensory and suitable analytical trend data under realistic storage |
| Physical damage | Flakes may become crumbs or lose visible identity | Distribution and package-abuse studies |
| Post-opening use | Repeated opening changes oxygen and humidity exposure | Defined instructions supported by study |

## Study outline for a qualified partner

Define the exact SKU, manufacturing process, intended ready-to-eat status, package, target markets, intended shelf life, and foreseeable storage conditions. Establish baseline microbiology, water activity, quality, and packaging data. Run appropriately designed real-time storage studies, with accelerated studies only where scientifically justified. Include worst-case batches and a distribution simulation. Set pass/fail limits before testing; document deviations and recalls/withdrawal procedures. Confirm the final label date mark and after-opening instructions with regulatory review.

The 12–24+ month number is a project target only. No numeric shelf-life claim or guaranteed storage temperature is supported by product data.
