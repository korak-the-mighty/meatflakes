## Differentiation hypothesis

The possible distinction is a **visible, discrete meat flake designed to finish ordinary meals**, with a texture that works dry and/or after contact with food. This is a positioning and product-performance hypothesis. It is not a claim that no competitor has done it, and it cannot support a patent claim on its own.

## Comparison protocol

Buy current products in the target country and record date, SKU, origin, actual ingredients, package size, price per serving, storage instructions before and after opening, nutrition, piece appearance, and application photos. Blindly compare each with a Meatflakes prototype in the same dishes and at equal meat mass. Add frozen/pre-cooked meat and freshly cooked meat as practical reference points.

## Open research

Search EU retailers and foodservice catalogs, not only US and Singapore examples. Identify real-meat toppings in chicken and beef, plus direct guanciale alternatives. Check whether any product explicitly promises both dry crunch and softening in moist food. Do not infer market share or sales from a product listing.
