## Questions for each introduction

1. Have you developed or tested shelf-stable ready-to-eat meat pieces or comparable products? Which part can you do directly and what must be referred out?
2. What would rule out a 12–24+ month unopened ambient target? Which short preliminary tests would inform that decision?
3. How would you assess water activity, pathogen control, oxidation, texture/rehydration, package barrier and post-opening use for lean and fatty variants?
4. What is the smallest useful paid feasibility scope, deliverable, estimated schedule and indicative price? What data or samples should we bring?
5. How will confidentiality, ownership of results and publication be handled?
