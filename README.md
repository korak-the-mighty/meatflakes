# Meatflakes

**Status: concept and research workspace · 5 October 2026**

Meatflakes is a proposed line of **shelf-stable, real-meat flakes or toppings** that people can add to everyday foods to turn them into a meal. The meat should remain visibly and recognizably real meat. The product is **not meat powder, jerky, or conventional meat floss**. Candidate variants include chicken, beef, and pork or guanciale. A crunchy dry version and a version that softens or reconstitutes in hot or moist food are possibilities to test. **Carbonara finished with guanciale flakes** is the defining use example.

This repository records the concept and the work needed to determine whether it is desirable, feasible, safe, compliant, and commercially viable. There is no finished formulation, validated shelf life, regulatory approval, patent opinion, or market forecast here.

## Evidence labels

- **Concept:** the intended product, not an established technical result.
- **Established:** a claim supported by a linked source, limited to what that source actually says.
- **Hypothesis:** something to test; never a claimed finding.
- **Decision:** a choice or proposed criterion for the project, subject to revision.

Product pages establish that a company offers or describes a product; they do not independently prove its safety or sales. US technical guidance helps frame experiments but does not replace EU food law or validation by a qualified food manufacturer.

## Current priorities

1. Define the first use case and test whether a visible meat topping changes how people perceive a simple dish.
2. Compare prototypes against bacon bits, jerky pieces, and pork floss in blind use tests.
3. Engage a qualified meat processor and food microbiologist for process and shelf-life design before any sale.
4. Map the EU product category, labeling, facility obligations, and patent landscape with specialists.
