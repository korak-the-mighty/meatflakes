# Meatflakes

**Status: concept / discovery. Last reviewed: 5 October 2026.** This public repository is the project's single source of truth. [Start with the action list](NEXT.md).

**The proposition:** real meat in visible, discrete flakes that can live in a pantry and be shaken or poured onto everyday food to help make a meal. It should be good straight from the pack. A crispy dry bite and pleasant softening in hot or moist food are product goals to test. Chicken, beef, pork and a guanciale-inspired carbonara use case are candidate directions. This is not meant to be powder, jerky or conventional fine meat floss.

**Evidence boundary:** There is no documented Meatflakes prototype, process validation, measured shelf life, customer result, production quote, patent opinion or approved pack claim. **12–24+ months unopened is an ambition for professional validation, not a product claim.** Ambient storage, ready-to-eat use and after-opening instructions depend on the final process and package. No unvalidated prototype should be sold or distributed as a shelf-stable food.

## How to read this repository

- **Concept / decision:** what the team wants to make or a proposed next step.
- **Verified external fact:** a narrow statement backed by a linked primary source, dated where relevant. It does not prove anything about a Meatflakes SKU.
- **Hypothesis / target:** an assertion to test. A target is never a shelf-life, safety, nutrition, sales or patent claim.
- **Measured result:** requires batch or study records; there are none yet.

The repository is public by the owner's choice. Record technical results carefully and obtain patent advice before publishing potentially novel process details. Existing product and patent examples are starting points, not a comprehensive search.
