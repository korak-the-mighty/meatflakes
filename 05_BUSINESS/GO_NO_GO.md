# Go / no-go gates

**Proposed decision framework, not claimed results.** Set numeric thresholds with experts and actual customer economics before a funded pilot. Record the evidence, owner, date and decision at every gate. A failure can mean revise one feature, narrow the market, or stop.

| Gate | Advance only if… | Evidence / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Kitchen desirability | At least one Prototype 001 variant is genuinely enjoyable and recognizable as meat on an everyday dish | Dated batch log, photos and scores; Henrik |
| 2. Technical plausibility | A qualified food technologist sees a credible route to safe ready-to-eat use, desired texture and industrial production | Written feasibility scope with main risks; expert |
| 3. Prior art and IP | Specific claims/process questions have been searched; any patent strategy or freedom-to-operate statement is reviewed professionally | Search log and counsel advice where needed |
| 4. Professional prototype | Focused pilot samples meet defined sensory and preliminary safety specifications under an appropriate food safety system | Pilot records and laboratory results; processor |
| 5. Shelf life | A process and package have a validated, evidence-based unopened life and after-opening instructions for the exact SKU | Qualified real-time/justified accelerated study and regulatory review |
| 6. Repeat use | Households reach for the product more than once and finish packs for real dishes at an acceptable price | [Consumer diary](../07_TESTING/CONSUMER_TEST.md) and observed purchase intent/action |
| 7. Economics | Measured yield and quotes support positive contribution in the chosen channel at the price customers accept | [Unit economics](UNIT_ECONOMICS.md) with sensitivity |

The **12–24+ month unopened target** is a question for Gate 5, not a condition already met. If a shorter but commercially useful life is the only feasible result, reassess the proposition and channel. If the product is merely liked as a garnish, do not call it meal-making without changing the serving size or positioning.

**Company decision:** proceed only when a safe product has repeat use and viable economics. Do not turn a positive kitchen tasting into a launch decision.
